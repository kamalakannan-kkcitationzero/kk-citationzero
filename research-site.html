About

I am an Assistant Professor in the MBA programme at S.A. Engineering College, Chennai. I completed my Ph.D. in Business Administration at the University of Madras (Department of Adult and Continuing Education) and have taught management since 2023, after an HR traineeship in the automobile industry. My research looks at human resource management, employee motivation and work efficiency, and the growing role of AI and digital transformation in HR, retail and higher education. I am the author of the book The Human Output: Bridging Motivation, Morale, and the Generation Gaps (2026), with 18 journal articles, book chapters and conference papers.

Research interests

HRM & employee motivation

Work culture, motivation, absenteeism, work efficiency and workplace neurodiversity, with a focus on the automobile and IT sectors.

AI in HR & business

AI in HR analytics and hiring, customer relationship management, and consumer behaviour in digital retailing.

Digital transformation & education

ICT, AI and digital transformation in higher education and teaching performance, plus researcher challenges.
